📰 Latest AI News — 22 November 2026

1. Sarvam AI Launches Upgraded Sarvam-105B-1711 Open-Weight Model

Published: 22 November 2026
Sources: "Sarvam AI" (https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-105b-1711) | "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/sarvam-hits-back-at-critics-amid-backlash-over-chinese-models-announces-improved-105b-ai-model/articleshow/136079350.cms)

Sarvam released Sarvam-105B-1711, an upgraded open-weight model designed for customer support, voice agents, and agentic workflows. The company reports an improvement from 42.6 to 73.9 across eight conversational-agent benchmarks and reduced blended inference pricing from $0.44 to $0.15 per million tokens.

Why it matters: Creates new opportunities for affordable Indian-language AI applications, voice agents, and tool-calling workflows.

2. OpenAI Proposes Shared AI Capability Limits Using Perplexity

Published: 22 November 2026
Sources: "OpenAI" (https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/) | "Reuters" (https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-scientist-proposes-industry-ai-limits-tied-model-intelligence-metric-2026-11-22/)

OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki proposed using perplexity and other measurements to establish shared AI capability standards. The proposal recommends that frontier AI developers agree on capability limits until monitoring and safety systems meet specific milestones.

Why it matters: Highlights the importance of model evaluation, perplexity, capability monitoring, red-teaming, and AI safety standards.

3. Google Reports a Major Shift Towards Autonomous AI-Powered Cyberattacks

Published: 22 November 2026
Sources: "TechRadar Pro" (https://www.techradar.com/pro/security/google-says-it-just-saw-the-first-fully-autonomous-cyberattack-and-says-a-significant-threshold-has-been-crossed-in-terms-of-cybersecurity) | "Google Cloud" (https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/agentic-threat-activity-crosses-a-significant-threshold)

Google researchers reported AI-linked systems being used to discover software vulnerabilities, generate exploits, and automate stages of cyber intrusions. The research describes agentic frameworks such as CAIRN and PentAGI being used in real-world attacks.

Why it matters: Reinforces the need for sandboxing, restricted tool permissions, monitoring, and human oversight in autonomous AI systems.

4. Banks Push Back Against Third-Party AI Agents Accessing Customer Accounts

Published: 22 November 2026
Source: "WIRED" (https://www.wired.com/story/banks-are-cracking-down-on-ai-agents/)

Major US financial institutions are tightening restrictions on third-party AI agents accessing customer accounts and performing transactions. Wells Fargo is updating its customer agreement to warn users about the risks of AI-agent activity.

Why it matters: Developers building browser-use and financial agents need scoped permissions, transaction confirmations, secure credential handling, and auditable actions.

5. Elon Musk Warns of a Potential AI Infrastructure Cash Crunch in 2027

Published: 22 November 2026
Source: "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/international/world-news/where-will-that-come-from-elon-musk-warns-ais-billion-dollar-race-may-hit-a-nuts-cash-wall-in-2027/articleshow/136076490.cms)

Elon Musk warned that AI companies could face a major cash shortage in 2027 if data-centre construction and infrastructure deployment are delayed. The warning highlights the growing costs of GPUs, computing facilities, cooling, and electricity.

Why it matters: Makes inference optimisation, quantisation, caching, and efficient model deployment increasingly important for AI engineers.
