## 📰 Latest AI News — 2 November 2026

### 1. Gulf Investors Explore India's AI Infrastructure Market
**Published:** 2 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/company/corporate-trends/gulf-investors-eye-indias-ai-infrastructure-and-space-tech-even-as-broader-interest-stays-subdued/articleshow/135435488.cms)

Gulf-based investors are exploring opportunities in India's AI infrastructure and space technology sectors. Data-centre operator Yotta raised $150 million earlier this year to expand its AI infrastructure.

**Why it matters:** Highlights growing investment opportunities in AI infrastructure, data centres, GPUs, and high-performance computing.

### 2. South Korea Plans Nearly $16 Billion Investment in AI
**Published:** 2 November 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-koreas-lee-vows-make-bold-ai-investment-record-budget-push-2026-11-02/)

South Korea plans to invest 21.3 trillion won (approximately $15.97 billion) in AI, semiconductors, physical AI, and AI data centres. The proposed allocation is nearly double the previous year's amount.

**Why it matters:** Shows the growing importance of physical AI, robotics, AI chips, and large-scale computing infrastructure.

### 3. DeepSeek Releases Open-Source Super-Agent to Challenge Claude Code
**Published:** 1 November 2026; covered 2 November  
**Source:** [The Star / Bloomberg](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/02/deepseek-drops-enhanced-coding-agent-to-rival-anthropic)

DeepSeek released an experimental open-source coding agent called Super-Agent. It can monitor developers' group chats for coding problems and proactively suggest solutions without waiting for direct prompts.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the evolution of agentic coding systems, proactive assistance, and autonomous developer workflows.

### 4. OpenAI Wants to Rebrand Data Centres as AI Compute Campuses
**Published:** 1 November 2026  
**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/01/openai-might-rebrand-its-data-centers-as-ai-compute-campuses/)

OpenAI is preparing a policy and public-relations campaign around AI infrastructure. Proposals reportedly include calling data centres "AI compute campuses" and promoting AI speed limits for the most powerful models.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the importance of compute infrastructure, energy efficiency, inference optimisation, and AI governance.

### 5. AI Investment Concerns Trigger a Shift in Global Investor Allocations
**Published:** 2 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/news/fpis-turn-net-buyers-as-ai-trade-worries-trigger-allocation-shift/articleshow/135441107.cms)

Foreign portfolio investors invested approximately $1.2 billion in Indian equities over ten trading sessions. Market participants suggest concerns about the global AI investment boom may have influenced the shift.

**Why it matters:** AI infrastructure spending and model monetisation are increasingly influencing global technology investment and business decisions.
