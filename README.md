## 📰 Latest AI News — 16 November 2026

### 1. Alibaba Launches Qwen4-Max with 2.4 Trillion Parameters and 1M Context Window
**Published:** 16 November 2026  
**Source:** [Alibaba Cloud](https://www.alibabacloud.com/en/press-room/alibaba-unveils-qwen4-max?_p_lc=1)

Alibaba launched Qwen4-Max, its new flagship AI model featuring 2.4 trillion total parameters, 71 billion activated parameters per token, native vision capabilities, and a one-million-token context window. Alibaba claims improved coding and agentic performance, using 35% fewer tokens in high-effort reasoning and reducing average task costs by around 60% on its Work360 benchmark.

**Why it matters:** Large-context models and stronger agentic capabilities can improve complex RAG systems, coding agents, and long-running AI workflows.

### 2. UN Climate Chief Warns About AI's Potential and Risks at COP31
**Published:** 16 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech_topic.cms?query=ArtificialIntelligence)

UN climate chief Simon Stiell described AI as a double-edged technology during COP31 discussions in Antalya, highlighting both its potential and risks for climate action. The discussion accompanied the launch of an AI pledge at the United Nations climate conference.

**Why it matters:** AI can support climate modelling and energy optimisation, but efficient computing and responsible AI deployment are increasingly important.

### 3. Apple Launches Health AI Research Study with OpenEvidence
**Published:** 15 November 2026  
**Source:** [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/11/apple-launches-research-study-to-explore-the-use-of-ai-for-health/)

Apple launched the Apple Health AI Usage Study in collaboration with OpenEvidence. The US-based research study will examine trust, comprehension, and real-world engagement with conversational health AI, including responses grounded in peer-reviewed medical research.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates domain-specific RAG, medical source grounding, privacy protection, and safety guardrails for AI applications.

### 4. Mirendil Raises $1 Billion to Automate AI Research with Agents
**Published:** 12 November 2026  
**Source:** [Mirendil](https://mirendil.com/news/series-a/)

AI research startup Mirendil announced a $1 billion Series A funding round. Its technology aims to automate AI research using agents that write code and conduct experiments. The company also introduced Fern, a model supporting up to 10 million tokens of context, and Lor, an agent orchestration system.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the growing role of multi-agent systems, automated experimentation, and AI-assisted model development.

### 5. OpenAI Introduces ChatGPT Wallet for AI-Agent Payments
**Published:** 13 November 2026  
**Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/)

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, enabling AI agents to make purchases without directly accessing users' actual card details. The system uses tokenised payment credentials and requires user approval and a security check for every purchase during its initial rollout.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the evolution of AI agents from information assistants into transactional systems, requiring secure integrations, consent mechanisms, and audit trails.
