📰 Latest AI News — 25 November 2026

1. AI Agents Set to Reshape Indian Workplaces as 93% of Leaders Plan Adoption

Published: 25 November 2026
Source: "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/ai-agents-set-to-reshape-indian-workplaces-as-93-of-leaders-plan-adoption-kpmg-cii-report/articleshow/136148551.cms)

A joint KPMG-CII report found that 93% of Indian business leaders plan to
adopt AI agents, while 59% already use them to automate workstreams or
business processes. The report highlights the growing importance of
AI direction, output evaluation, and human oversight.

Why it matters: Growing demand for production-ready AI agents,
business automation, tool integration, and human-in-the-loop systems.

2. Anthropic Adds Skills Over MCP, Background Tasks and Interactive Workflows

Published: 24 November 2026
Source: "Anthropic" (https://claude.com/resources/articles/skills-over-mcp-tasks-and-more)

Anthropic introduced Skills over MCP in public beta, allowing MCP servers
to provide reusable instructions alongside tools. Tasks now support
long-running background operations, while Elicitation enables servers
to request user input and approval during workflows.

Why it matters: Important developments for agent architecture,
MCP integrations, context management, asynchronous execution, and
human approval workflows.

3. OpenAI Reports GPT-6 Models Can Design Proteins Using Reasoning and Code

Published: 24 November 2026
Source: "OpenAI" (https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities/)

OpenAI reported that GPT-6 models can design protein structures and
sequences using reasoning and general-purpose code. Laboratory tests
found evidence of binding in 35 out of 322 tested minibinder candidates
across five targets. The research is an early preprint result.

Why it matters: Demonstrates how general-purpose reasoning models,
code execution, and specialised tools can work together for scientific
discovery.

4. Google DeepMind Introduces Humanity's First Exam to Test LLM Reliability

Published: 24 November 2026
Source: "Google DeepMind" (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/humanitys-first-exam/)

Google DeepMind launched Humanity's First Exam, a benchmark containing
544 text and image prompts across 20 foundational capabilities.
It evaluates whether AI models can consistently answer simple,
everyday questions. A Kaggle competition invites community submissions.

Why it matters: Highlights the importance of evaluating LLM reliability,
consistency, and multimodal understanding beyond traditional benchmarks.

5. Salesforce Leadership Highlights Small Language Models and AI ROI

Published: 25 November 2026
Source: "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/ai/ai-insights/articlelist/121648923.cms)

At the ET Future of Knowledge Work Summit 2026, Salesforce South Asia
leadership highlighted the cost-effectiveness of small language models
for targeted business use cases. The discussion emphasised practical
ROI and choosing efficient AI solutions instead of using large models
for every task.

Why it matters: Model selection, inference cost optimisation, and
efficient deployment are important skills for production AI engineers.
