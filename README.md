📰 Latest AI News — 26 November 2026

1. India Launches Chariot-1 Indigenous Foundation AI Models

Published: 26 November 2026
Source: "PIB — IndiaAI Mission" (https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2341725&lang=1&reg=3)

India launched Chariot-1 Foundation Models under the IndiaAI Mission. The models support English and 10 Indian languages, with multilingual processing and speech-generation capabilities. Its reported innovations include IndicShift, Nested Training, and Mixture of Vocabularies.

Why it matters: Highlights progress in sovereign AI, multilingual LLMs, speech generation, and efficient model training.

2. Agentic AI Adoption by Global Banks Could Pressure Indian IT Revenues

Published: 26 November 2026
Source: "ETCIO" (https://cio.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/artificial-intelligence/agentic-ai-adoption-by-global-banks-could-put-further-pressure-on-indian-it-revenues/136184033)

A Kotak Institutional Equities report says global banks are increasingly adopting agentic AI to improve productivity and reduce operational costs. Some financial services firms report productivity gains of 20–40%.

Why it matters: Creates demand for enterprise AI agents, workflow automation, RAG, API integrations, and measurable AI productivity improvements.

3. Nvidia Halts AI Server Shipments to Four Southeast Asian Countries

Published: 26 November 2026
Source: "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-halts-chip-shipments-to-southeast-asia-ft-reports/articleshow/136180352.cms)

Nvidia reportedly instructed manufacturing partners to pause shipments of servers containing its AI chips to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia amid tighter US export controls.

Why it matters: Shows how GPU availability, export restrictions, and data-centre capacity affect AI training and inference infrastructure.

4. US Chip Restrictions and Distillation Safeguards Could Delay Chinese AI Models

Published: 26 November 2026
Source: "Reuters" (https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-chip-curbs-distillation-crackdown-threaten-chinese-ai-model-development-2026-11-26/)

US restrictions on advanced computing access and stronger safeguards against model distillation could delay Z.AI's GLM-5.5 and Moonshot AI's Kimi K4.

Why it matters: Highlights the importance of compute-efficient architectures, model distillation, training infrastructure, and inference optimisation.

5. India's AI Learning Boom Puts Human Skills into Focus

Published: 26 November 2026
Source: "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/AI/news)

An ET report highlights growing demand for AI skills alongside human capabilities such as critical thinking and complex problem-solving. India ranked 40th in the report's global comparison.

Why it matters: AI engineers need strong problem-solving, debugging, system design, and evaluation skills in addition to LLM knowledge.

Each news entry should include:

- **Headline:** What happened?
