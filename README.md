## 📰 Latest AI News — 7 November 2026

### 1. India Releases Three Indigenous AI Foundation Models
**ET coverage:** 7 November 2026  
**Original announcement:** 3 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/eka-1-dhir-2b-and-dhir-5b-govt-launches-three-indigenous-artificial-intelligence-foundation-models/videoshow/135618717.cms)

IndiaAI Mission-backed teams introduced EKA-1 by Soket Labs and Dhīr 2B and Dhīr 5B by ZenteiQ. EKA-1 supports 22 scheduled Indian languages and English, while the Dhīr models are designed for secure, multilingual, offline-capable deployment.

**Why it matters:** Creates opportunities for multilingual LLM applications, local inference, private RAG systems, and AI solutions for Indian languages.

### 2. OpenAI Partners with Hacktron to Discover Security Vulnerabilities
**Published:** 6 November 2026  
**Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/partnering-with-hacktron/)

OpenAI partnered with cybersecurity company Hacktron to identify vulnerabilities in its systems, focusing on interactions with third-party services. Hacktron will receive expanded access to frontier models through Daybreak Red.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the growing role of AI agents in automated code review, penetration testing, and cybersecurity research.

### 3. India's Coforge Joins SpaceX Partner Network to Deploy Grok in Enterprises
**Published:** 6 November 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/indias-coforge-joins-spacex-partner-network-deploy-grok-ai-tools-enterprises-2026-11-06/)

Coforge joined the SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network to help enterprises integrate Grok models and tools into business workflows through its Forward Deployed Engineering unit.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the transition from AI experimentation to production deployment and the growing demand for applied AI engineering.

### 4. Google DeepMind Uses AI Agents to Find Weaknesses in Legacy RSA Encryption
**Published:** 6 November 2026  
**Source:** [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/)

Google DeepMind shared research showing that human-guided AI agents uncovered two weaknesses in legacy 1024-bit RSA cryptosystems.

**Why it matters:** Shows how AI agents can accelerate cryptographic research and cybersecurity while reinforcing the need to modernise legacy encryption.

### 5. Anthropic's IPO Plans Highlight the Growing Economics of Frontier AI
**Latest coverage:** 7 November 2026  
**SEC filing:** 2 November 2026  
**Source:** [SEC Registration Filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026068676/anthropicpbc-sx1ax1.htm)

Anthropic's proposed public listing has drawn attention to the financial scale of frontier AI development. Its SEC filing describes major infrastructure commitments, business risks, and plans to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the importance of compute efficiency, inference optimisation, infrastructure costs, and sustainable AI business models.
