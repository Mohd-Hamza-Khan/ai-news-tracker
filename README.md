📰 Latest AI News — 23 November 2026

1. Google Introduces File-Slicing Controls in the Gemini API

Published: 23 November 2026
Source: "Google AI" (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/file-slicing-controls/)

Google introduced native file-slicing controls in the Gemini API. Developers can specify selected PDF pages, spreadsheet ranges, video segments, and audio sections for model processing. This avoids sending entire large files when only specific portions are needed.

Why it matters: Helps AI engineers build more efficient document RAG pipelines and multimodal agents while reducing unnecessary token consumption.

2. Anthropic Launches Claude Ship for Automated Code Review and Approvals

Published: 23 November 2026
Source: "Anthropic" (https://claude.com/resources/articles/introducing-claude-ship)

Anthropic introduced Claude Ship, an AI-agent suite that reviews pull requests, fixes code issues, and approves changes according to team-defined policies. Its new beta agents include PR Steward and Claude Approvals.

Why it matters: Shows how AI coding agents are evolving from code generation toward automated software delivery, CI/CD workflows, and repository management.

3. OpenAI and Cisco Introduce Shirudo, an Open-Source AI Security Project

Published: 23 November 2026
Source: "OpenAI" (https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo/)

OpenAI and Cisco introduced Shirudo, an open-source runtime that uses eBPF to protect running Linux systems while permanent security patches are prepared. AI agents can help identify vulnerabilities, generate temporary shields, and test protections before deployment.

Why it matters: Demonstrates practical applications of agentic AI in cybersecurity, infrastructure protection, and automated vulnerability mitigation.

4. India AI Startup Funding Nearly Doubles to $1.6 Billion

Published: 23 November 2026
Source: "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/india-ai-funding-nearly-doubles-to-usd-1-6-billion-in-jan-sept-2026-tracxn/articleshow/136102647.cms)

According to Tracxn, Indian AI companies raised $1.6 billion in equity funding during January–September 2026, a 96% increase compared with the same period in 2025. Funding was raised across 87 rounds.

Why it matters: Highlights growing investment and opportunities in India's AI startup ecosystem, enterprise AI, and AI infrastructure.

5. PwC India Launches Future AI Experience Lab with 90+ AI Solutions

Published: 23 November 2026
Source: "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/pwc-india-launches-ai-lab-in-gurugram-with-over-90-solutions-across-sectors/articleshow/136100231.cms)

PwC India launched its Future AI Experience Lab in Gurugram, featuring more than 90 validated AI solutions across manufacturing, healthcare, infrastructure, telecommunications, and banking. The facility supports business simulations and AI prototyping.

Why it matters: Shows the growing demand for practical enterprise AI applications, agentic workflows, robotics, and measurable business automation.
