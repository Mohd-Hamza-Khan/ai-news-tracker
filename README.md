## 📰 Latest AI News — 13 November 2026

### 1. Google Launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen for Agentic Workflows
**Published:** 12 November 2026  
**Source:** [Google DeepMind](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/)

Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast model designed for coding, everyday tasks, and agentic workflows. It is available through the Gemini API, AI Studio, Gemini app, and Google Search. Google reports an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score of 51 at its default high-thinking level.

**Why it matters:** Faster models can improve agent execution and reduce the cost of multi-model AI workflows.

### 2. OpenAI Brings Local API Inference to India with GPT-6 Luna
**Published:** 12 November 2026; ET follow-up: 13 November  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://datacenters.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/ai-compute-infrastructure/openai-offers-local-api-data-processing-for-indian-firms/135786387)

OpenAI has made local inference residency available for supported GPT-6 Luna API workloads in India. Eligible organisations can process supported requests and model inference within the country, helping meet data residency requirements.

**Why it matters:** Enables more enterprise AI applications where data sovereignty, privacy, and regulatory compliance are important.

### 3. SGLang Consortium Brings Major AI Companies Together for Open-Source Inference
**Published:** 12 November 2026  
**Source:** [LMSYS Org](https://www.lmsys.org/blog/2026-11-12-sglang-consortium/)

LMSYS Org launched the SGLang Consortium with NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, Google, Meta, Cloudflare, Modal, and RadixArk. The collaboration aims to advance open-source inference, distributed workloads, multimodal support, and hardware optimisation.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the growing importance of inference optimisation, GPU utilisation, and open-source AI infrastructure.

### 4. Nonprofits Sue OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, and Meta Over Frontier AI Development
**Published:** 12 November 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-google-anthropic-hit-with-lawsuit-seeking-halt-frontier-ai-development-2026-11-12/)

A coalition of nonprofits filed a lawsuit in California seeking a court order to halt development of advanced AI models by major AI companies. The complaint alleges unfair business practices and public-health risks. These remain allegations, not established findings or an existing development ban.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the increasing importance of AI safety, governance, transparency, and responsible deployment.

### 5. SMIC Warns AI Chip Supply Constraints May Continue Through 2027
**Published:** 13 November 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/smic-says-ai-chip-supply-chain-will-remain-tight-through-2027-2026-11-13/)

SMIC said strong AI-chip demand could keep semiconductor supply chains tight through 2027. The company's management cited demand from data centres and private cloud infrastructure.

**Why it matters:** Semiconductor capacity and hardware availability directly affect AI infrastructure costs, model deployment, and GPU planning.
