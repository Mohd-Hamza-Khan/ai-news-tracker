## 📰 Latest AI News — 15 November 2026

### 1. AI-Related Debt Reaches $687 Billion as Big Tech Spending Accelerates
**Published:** 14 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/ai/ai-insights/ai-debt-hits-687-billion-as-big-techs-funding-appetite-soars/articleshow/135824839.cms)

A Goldman Sachs report estimates that global AI-related debt reached approximately $687 billion by November 3, 2026. Five major hyperscalers issued $296 billion in debt during the year, while also carrying significant lease and purchase commitments.

**Why it matters:** AI infrastructure costs influence GPU availability, cloud pricing, inference economics, and the commercial viability of AI applications.

### 2. AI Becomes a Major Concern for Financial-System Stability, Fed Survey Finds
**Published:** 14 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/world/ai-is-now-wall-streets-biggest-nightmare-87-of-experts-warn-of-risks-to-financial-system-fed-survey-finds/articleshow/135824907.cms)

A Federal Reserve survey found that 87% of respondents identified AI as a major risk to financial stability. Concerns include AI-enabled cyberattacks, financial-market disruption, and increasing exposure to AI infrastructure investments.

**Why it matters:** AI engineers working in fintech and financial services need to prioritise security, reliability, explainability, and risk management.

### 3. OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet for AI-Agent Payments
**Published:** 13 November 2026  
**Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/ml-IN/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/)

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet, enabling AI agents to make purchases on behalf of users without accessing their actual card details. Users review and approve each transaction through a security check. The initial rollout is limited to eligible US ChatGPT Pro subscribers.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the growth of agentic commerce and creates opportunities for secure AI shopping agents, payment integrations, and human-in-the-loop workflows.

### 4. Cloudflare Open-Sources Kitesurf, a Browser Built for AI Agents
**Published:** 13 November 2026  
**Source:** [Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kitesurf-open-source/)

Cloudflare open-sourced Kitesurf under the Apache 2.0 licence. It is a lightweight browser engine for AI agents that supports JavaScript execution, screenshots, Chrome DevTools Protocol, and Puppeteer integration.

**Why it matters:** Provides developers with new infrastructure for browser-use agents, web automation, and autonomous research applications.

### 5. LangChain Raises $300 Million at a $3 Billion Valuation
**Published:** 13 November 2026  
**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/ai-agent-startup-langchain-hits-3b-valuation/)

LangChain raised $300 million in a Series C funding round at a $3 billion valuation, led by CapitalG. The company is expanding its enterprise AI-agent infrastructure through LangSmith, which supports agent development, evaluation, monitoring, and management.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the growing demand for production-ready AI-agent infrastructure, observability, evaluation, and deployment tools.
