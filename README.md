## 📰 Latest AI News — 4 November 2026

### 1. Mistral Releases Open Weights for Its 1-Trillion-Parameter Large 4 Model
**Published:** 4 November 2026  
**Source:** [Mistral AI](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/)

Mistral released the weights for Mistral Large 4, a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model with approximately 1 trillion total parameters and 49 billion active parameters. It targets agentic coding, reasoning, and defensive cybersecurity.

**Why it matters:** Gives developers more control over model deployment, fine-tuning, inference infrastructure, and private AI applications.

### 2. IndiaAI Mission Releases Three Indigenous Foundation Models
**Published:** 3 November 2026  
**Source:** [Press Information Bureau](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2332315&lang=1&reg=3)

India's AI Mission announced EKA-1 by Soket and Dhīr 2B and Dhīr 5B by ZenteiQ. EKA-1 has 26.3 billion parameters and supports all 22 scheduled Indian languages and English. The Dhīr models are multilingual assistants designed for secure, offline-capable deployment.

**Why it matters:** Strengthens India's sovereign AI ecosystem, multilingual AI development, and private local model deployment.

### 3. CERT-In Gets Access to Anthropic's Mythos 5 for Cybersecurity Testing
**Published:** 4 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/cert-in-gets-mythos-access-being-careful-with-testing-officials/articleshow/135507793.cms)

India's cybersecurity agency CERT-In received access to Anthropic's Mythos 5 model to explore its cybersecurity applications. Officials are cautious because the cloud-based access requires sensitive information to be sent to external servers rather than allowing fully air-gapped local deployment.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the importance of data privacy, secure inference, and local deployment for sensitive AI workloads.

### 4. CrowdStrike Acquires AI Startups Fiddler and Dreadnode
**Published:** 3 November 2026  
**Source:** [CrowdStrike — Fiddler acquisition](https://ir.crowdstrike.com/news-releases/news-release-details/crowdstrike-acquire-fiddler-deliver-agent-observability-ai-trust/)

CrowdStrike agreed to acquire Fiddler AI for agent observability and Dreadnode for automated AI security testing. The combined deal was reported at approximately $495 million. The technology will help enterprises evaluate, monitor, and secure AI agents.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates growing demand for AI-agent observability, behavioural monitoring, security evaluation, and runtime protection.

### 5. Kimi K3.1 Pro: Moonshot's New Open-Weight Model
**Published:** Model announcement before 4 November 2026  
**Source:** [Moonshot AI](https://www.kimi.com/en/blog/kimi-k3-1-pro)

Moonshot introduced Kimi K3.1 Pro, a 3-trillion-class open-weight model with a one-million-token context window. It targets coding, 3D creation, financial research, and agentic workflows. Full model weights were scheduled for release by 4 November; public availability of the weights was not independently confirmed.

**Why it matters:** Long-context open-weight models can support private codebase analysis, document intelligence, and customised agentic applications.
