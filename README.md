## 📰 Latest AI News — 12 November 2026

### 1. Enterprises Shift Towards Open-Weight AI Models to Control Costs
**Published:** 12 November 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/most-large-firms-plan-higher-open-weight-ai-use-cap-bills-study-shows-2026-11-12/)

A Capgemini study found that 59% of surveyed executives at large companies plan to increase their use of open-weight AI models over the next year. The study estimates that open-weight tokens cost 87% less on average than closed-model tokens.

**Why it matters:** Open-weight models provide opportunities to reduce inference costs, improve deployment flexibility, and build private AI systems.

### 2. Google Launches Gemini Decisions API for Fast AI Decisions
**Announced:** 11 November 2026  
**Source:** [64bit](https://64bit.co.uk/posts/daily-brief-2026-11-12)

Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API, powered by Gemini Decision 1. It evaluates up to 1,000 yes/no, multiple-choice, or rubric-based questions against shared multimodal input and returns confidence scores.

**Why it matters:** Could improve the speed and cost efficiency of classification, routing, moderation, evaluation, and AI-agent tool selection.

### 3. Google DeepMind Introduces AlphaGenome 2 for Cell-Level DNA Analysis
**Announced:** 11 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1031524/google-deepmind-alphagenome-2-genetic-ai-model)

Google DeepMind introduced AlphaGenome 2, a model that predicts how genetic variations affect specific cell types and biological states. It was trained on more than 100 million cell profiles across 68 tissues and is available for non-commercial research through an API.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the potential of AI in genomics, scientific discovery, disease research, and computational biology.

### 4. Alibaba Announces Qwen4 and Opens Qwen4-Max Preview
**Announced:** 11 November 2026  
**Source:** [AIBriefs](https://aibriefs.news/briefing/2026-11-12)

Alibaba's Qwen team announced its next-generation Qwen4 model family and opened a preview of Qwen4-Max. Reports describe advanced reasoning, coding, agentic workflows, and multimodal understanding capabilities.

**Why it matters:** Expands the model choices available to engineers building AI agents, coding assistants, and multimodal applications.

### 5. BCG Ranks India as an AI Rising Contender, Citing Its Talent Pool
**Published:** 11 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/india-artificial-intelligence)

A Boston Consulting Group report identified India as an AI Rising Contender, highlighting its skilled AI workforce, returning global talent, and AI training initiatives.

**Why it matters:** Highlights opportunities for Indian AI engineers in AI development, enterprise implementation, model deployment, and startups.
