📰 Latest AI News — 11 November 2026

1. Google Launches Gemini 4 Carbon for Advanced AI Agents

Published: 10 November 2026
Source: "Google DeepMind" (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)

Google made Gemini 4 Carbon available to developers, enterprises, scientists and consumers. The model targets complex coding, debugging, scientific research and long-horizon agentic workflows. Google also previewed "/swarm" in Antigravity to coordinate multiple AI agents from a single prompt.

Why it matters: Highlights advances in agent orchestration, long-horizon reasoning and autonomous software development.

2. Google DeepMind Introduces AlphaProof Code to Verify Signal's Security

Published: 10 November 2026
Source: "Google DeepMind" (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)

Google DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, an agentic framework that uses AI-generated Lean proofs to help verify selected security properties of Signal's cryptographic protocol implementation.

Why it matters: Demonstrates the potential of AI agents in formal verification, software security and automated code auditing.

3. Anthropic Introduces Claude Sheets, Meetings and Whiteboards

Published: 10 November 2026
Source: "Reuters" (https://www.reuters.com/technology/anthropic-brings-claude-google-meet-tackle-tasks-live-2026-11-10/)

Anthropic introduced Claude Sheets for spreadsheet workflows, Claude Meetings for live Google Meet collaboration, and Claude Whiteboards for organising discussions into actionable plans.

Why it matters: Expands the role of AI assistants into enterprise productivity, workflow automation and collaborative agents.

4. OpenAI Launches Businesses in ChatGPT and AI Agents for E-commerce

Published: 10 November 2026
Source: "OpenAI" (https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-in-chatgpt/)

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta, allowing companies to claim and manage business profiles, update information and monitor user engagement. It also introduced AI agents for US e-commerce businesses.

Why it matters: Creates new opportunities for conversational commerce, business integrations and customer-facing AI agents.

5. AI Training Startup Mercor Reaches $20 Billion Valuation

Published: 10 November 2026
Source: "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/ai-works)

AI training startup Mercor reportedly raised $750 million in a Series D funding round, reaching a $20 billion valuation. Investors included Benchmark, Felicis, Nvidia and funds managed by Apollo affiliates.

Why it matters: Highlights the importance of training data, human feedback and evaluation infrastructure in the AI ecosystem.

- **Headline:** What happened?
