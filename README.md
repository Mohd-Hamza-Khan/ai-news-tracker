📰 Latest AI News — 21 November 2026

1. Thinking Machines Releases Inkling 2 Mini — An Open-Weight AI Model for Laptops

Published: 20 November 2026 (covered on 21 November)
Source: "Thinking Machines Lab" (https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/)

Thinking Machines Lab released Inkling 2 Mini, a 26.4-billion-parameter open-weight model designed to run locally with an approximately 20 GB memory footprint. Its looped transformer architecture processes its middle layers multiple times per token to improve reasoning and agentic coding capabilities.

Why it matters: Highlights advances in local AI deployment, efficient inference, open-weight models, and agentic coding.

2. Apple Plans an Autonomous AI Agent to Compete With OpenAI, Google and Meta

Published: 21 November 2026
Source: "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/ai/ai-insights/apple-enters-ai-agent-race-late-bets-on-iphone-ecosystem-to-challenge-rivals-iphone-microsoft-muse-open-ai-chatgpt-google/articleshow/136048033.cms)

Apple is reportedly developing an autonomous AI agent capable of performing tasks across applications, browsers, and devices. The planned system could browse the internet, write and debug code, and coordinate specialised sub-agents to complete multi-step workflows.

Why it matters: Shows the shift from conversational AI to autonomous agents capable of planning, tool calling, and executing complex tasks.

3. Signal Restricts Screenshots to Prevent AI Agents From Reading Private Messages

Published: 20 November 2026 (covered on 21 November)
Source: "Reuters" (https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/signal-restricts-screenshots-block-ai-agents-reading-messages-2026-11-20/)

Signal is introducing default screenshot restrictions to prevent AI agents integrated into mobile operating systems from accessing private conversations. The company warns that screen-reading and accessibility permissions can expose sensitive information outside encrypted applications.

Why it matters: Highlights the importance of permission controls, sandboxing, user consent, and privacy safeguards in computer-use AI agents.

4. Databricks Reportedly Explores Funding at a $225 Billion Valuation

Published: 20 November 2026 (ET coverage: 21 November)
Source: "Reuters" (https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/databricks-early-talks-raise-funds-225-billion-valuation-wsj-reports-2026-11-20/)

Databricks is reportedly in early discussions to raise several billion dollars in a funding round that could value the AI and data software company at approximately $225 billion. Its products support enterprise data management, analytics, and AI application development.

Why it matters: Demonstrates the growing importance of enterprise data infrastructure, analytics, and production-ready AI platforms.

5. Raghuram Rajan Questions the AI Investment Race and Slow Enterprise Adoption

Published: 21 November 2026
Source: "The Economic Times" (https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/ai/ai-insights/raghuram-rajan-warns-ai-firms-data-centre-slowdown-highlights-risks-in-race-for-advanced-models/articleshow/136055696.cms)

Former RBI Governor Raghuram Rajan questioned whether massive investments in advanced AI models and data centres will produce lasting competitive advantages. He highlighted the gap between rapid AI progress and practical adoption by mainstream businesses.

Why it matters: Reinforces the importance of building reliable AI solutions that solve real business problems and deliver measurable value.
