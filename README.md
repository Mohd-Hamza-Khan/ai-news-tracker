## 📰 Latest AI News — 30 October 2026

### 1. Mistral Releases Large 4 — Open-Weight Model for Coding and Cybersecurity
**Published:** 29 October 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/mistral-ai-publicly-releases-large-4-targets-cybersecurity-users-2026-10-29/)

Mistral released the weights of Large 4, nicknamed Le Chonk. The model targets agentic coding and cybersecurity, and can be deployed on an organisation's own infrastructure.

**Why it matters:** Open-weight models offer developers greater control over deployment, customisation, data privacy, and inference infrastructure.

### 2. Anthropic Partners With Six Drug Developers and Starts Its Own Drug Discovery Work
**Published:** 29 October 2026  
**Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news)

Anthropic announced research partnerships with six organisations developing medicines, alongside its own drug-discovery initiative. The work explores how Claude can support different stages of drug research and development.

**Why it matters:** Shows how LLMs are expanding into scientific research and complex real-world workflows.

### 3. OpenAI Offers Up to $500 Monthly API Credits to Eligible ChatGPT Subscribers
**Published:** 29 October 2026  
**Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/)

Eligible ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers can claim up to $500 per month in API credits to build applications and AI agents.

**Why it matters:** Provides eligible developers with additional resources to experiment with API-powered products and agentic applications.

### 4. AI Agents Help Formalise a Mathematical Proof in Around 43 Million Lines
**Reported:** 30 October 2026  
**Source:** [AI News Daily](https://agihunt.info/en/daily/2026-10-30)

Axiom is using AI agents to formalise the Classification of Finite Simple Groups, a major mathematical result. The reported formalisation is around 43 million lines and requires machine verification of each step.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the potential of AI agents for complex mathematical reasoning and machine-verified technical work.

### 5. AI-Powered Cyberattacks Put Indian NBFCs Under Pressure
**Published:** 29 October 2026  
**Source:** [ETtech](https://m.economictimes.com/tech/newsletters/tech-top-5/cyberattacks-hit-nbfcs-pine-labs-q2-profit-zooms/articleshow/135314211.cms)

ETtech reported cyberattacks targeting smaller Indian NBFCs, with attackers reportedly using specialised AI toolkits. The report highlights substantial financial losses and numerous complaints.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces the need for secure authentication, API protection, anomaly detection, and monitoring in financial applications.
