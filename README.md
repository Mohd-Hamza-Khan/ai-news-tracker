## 📰 Latest AI News — 25 October 2026

### 1. Moonshot AI Limits Cyberattack Capabilities in Its Upcoming Kimi Model
**Published:** 24 October 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/moonshot-curbs-hacking-ability-new-kimi-model-fears-grow-over-ai-powered-2026-10-24/)

Moonshot says it reduced cyber-offensive capabilities in its upcoming open-weight Kimi model while attempting to preserve general coding performance. The company's internal benchmark results have not been independently verified.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the trade-offs between open-weight AI capabilities, cybersecurity safeguards, and responsible model releases.

### 2. Block Introduces Dessa, a Speech-to-Speech AI Model
**Published:** 24 October 2026  
**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/24/blocks-new-voice-model-sounds-eerily-human/)

Block introduced Dessa, an experimental voice model trained entirely on human speech. It supports full-duplex conversations and is designed to run locally on powerful consumer hardware.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates an alternative to traditional speech pipelines and highlights the potential of real-time, emotionally aware voice interfaces.

### 3. NCERT Introduces AI and Computational Thinking for Classes 9–12
**Published:** 25 October 2026  
**Source:** [The New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Oct/25/ethical-boundaries-to-detecting-deepfakes-ncert-introduces-20-hour-ai-module-for-classes-9-12)

NCERT introduced a 20-hour introductory module covering computational thinking, AI fundamentals, deepfakes, misinformation, fairness, and data privacy. It was developed in consultation with IIT Madras and IIT Jodhpur.

**Why it matters:** Expands AI literacy and responsible technology education in Indian schools.

### 4. 88% of Indian Enterprises Expect Demand for AI-Focused Engineers to Rise by 2030
**Published:** 23 October 2026  
**Source:** [The Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/technology/artificial-intelligence/forward-deployed-engineers-it-jobs-scaler-cmr-report-10933175/lite/)

A Scaler–CMR survey found that around 88% of surveyed Indian enterprises expect demand for forward-deployed engineers to rise by 2030. The role combines applied AI, software engineering, production deployment, and customer-facing problem-solving.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces the value of building, deploying, and maintaining real-world AI applications.

### 5. OpenAI Strengthens Security for Frontier AI Training
**Published:** 23 October 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-resumes-frontier-ai-training-with-enhanced-security-no-internet-access-2026-10-23/)

OpenAI said it resumed frontier model training after strengthening security and restricting internet access. AI-agent testing also uncovered a vulnerability in the Brotli compression tool.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the importance of sandboxing, least-privilege permissions, audit logs, and adversarial testing for AI systems.
