## 📰 Latest AI News — 18 November 2026

### 1. OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol
**Published:** 18 November 2026  
**Source:** [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/)

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.2 Astra for complex professional work and GPT-6.2 Sol for everyday tasks. Both models offer improvements in coding, reasoning, agentic workflows, and token efficiency.

**Why it matters:** Better reasoning and token efficiency can improve production AI applications, coding agents, and long-running workflows.

### 2. Siemens Launches Eigen Engineering Agent in India
**Published:** 18 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/small-biz/sme-sector/siemens-launches-eigen-engineering-agent-in-india-targets-smes/articleshow/135957987.cms)

Siemens launched its Eigen Engineering Agent in India to automate industrial engineering tasks such as PLC coding, HMI visualisation, and device configuration. The system has been piloted with more than 16 Indian customers.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates how agentic AI can execute specialised engineering workflows instead of simply generating text or suggestions.

### 3. IIT Kanpur and Bharat1.ai Launch AI Kavach Cybersecurity Lab
**Published:** 18 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/ai/ai-insights/iit-kanpur-bharat1-ai-launch-ai-cybersecurity-lab-to-secure-financial-systems/articleshow/135956192.cms)

IIT Kanpur and Bharat1.ai established AI Kavach to strengthen cybersecurity in India's financial sector. The lab will focus on adversarial testing, AI assurance, traceability, and sandboxing autonomous agents. It aims to train more than 300 students and professionals annually.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the importance of AI security, red-teaming, sandboxing, and responsible agent deployment.

### 4. 92% of Indian Organisations Report AI-Agent Security Incidents
**Published:** 18 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/ai/ai-insights/majority-of-indian-firms-hit-by-ai-agent-incident-in-past-12-months-only-36-contained-it-in-24-hrs-survey/articleshow/135958626.cms)

A ServiceNow survey of 500 security leaders found that 92% of surveyed Indian organisations experienced an AI-agent security incident in the previous 12 months. Only 36% could contain an incident within 24 hours, while just 19% had a designated person accountable for agentic AI security.

**Why it matters:** Reinforces the need for agent identity, restricted tool permissions, audit logs, human approval, and continuous monitoring.

### 5. Physical AI Startup Humyn Labs Raises $47 Million
**Published:** 18 November 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/ai/ai-insights/physical-ai-startup-humyn-labs-raises-47-million-founder-manish-agarwal-says-data-collection-is-not-the-real-game/articleshow/135949247.cms)

Physical AI startup Humyn Labs raised $47 million through equity and venture debt to expand its robotics and real-world AI operations. The company plans to invest in deployment centres, capture hardware, and data-processing infrastructure.

**Why it matters:** Shows growing investment in physical AI, robotics, computer vision, and real-world machine-learning applications.
