## 📰 Latest AI News — 27 October 2026

### 1. Indian Startup Uses AI to Develop More Energy-Efficient AI Chips
**Published:** 27 October 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/world/business/eye-for-an-eye-think-ai-for-ai-how-one-startup-is-using-ai-to-make-ai-chips-more-energy-efficient/articleshow/135208414.cms)

Discovered Materials, an Indian startup backed by $9 million in seed
funding from Lightspeed India Partners, is using AI to develop materials
intended to improve chip energy efficiency and thermal management.

**Why it matters:** More efficient chip materials could help reduce
the energy and cooling costs of AI infrastructure.

### 2. South Korea Warns of Chip Export Risks if AI Spending Slows
**Published:** 27 October 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/bank-korea-warns-countrys-chip-exports-could-fall-by-up-100-billion-if-ai-2026-10-27/)

The Bank of Korea modelled a scenario in which South Korean semiconductor
exports could fall $50–100 billion below earlier forecasts if major US
technology companies reduce AI infrastructure spending next year.

**Why it matters:** AI infrastructure investment affects chipmakers,
cloud providers, data centres, and the wider AI supply chain.

### 3. Anthropic Files for IPO, Revealing Its Enterprise AI Strategy
**Published:** 26 October 2026  
**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-officially-files-for-its-public-debut/)

Anthropic filed for an initial public offering under the planned ticker
ANT. Its filing reported $16.3 billion in revenue for the first half of
2026 and described an enterprise strategy spanning Claude models,
developer tools, and AI applications.

**Why it matters:** Shows how AI companies are combining models, APIs,
coding agents, and workplace applications into enterprise platforms.

### 4. Tata Communications Plans ₹4,350 Crore AI Connectivity Network
**Published:** 26 October 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/telecom/telecom-news/tata-communications-to-build-ai-super-highway-with-rs-4350-crore-investment-data-centre/articleshow/135207029.cms)

Tata Communications plans to invest ₹4,350 crore in approximately
10,000 km of AI-ready connectivity infrastructure across India,
connecting major data-centre corridors in phases.

**Why it matters:** Low-latency, high-capacity connectivity is essential
for scaling cloud platforms, distributed computing, and AI workloads.

### 5. AI Could Help Modernise Legacy COBOL Software
**Published:** 26 October 2026  
**Source:** [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1018434/cobol-migration-legacy-code-openai-anthropic)

OpenAI reported progress using AI models to translate legacy COBOL code
into modern programming languages. Experts cautioned that production
system migration still requires comprehensive testing and validation.

**Why it matters:** AI-assisted code migration could accelerate enterprise
modernisation, but behavioural preservation and human verification remain
essential.
