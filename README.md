## 📰 Latest AI News — 28 October 2026

### 1. OpenAI Reportedly Plans to Buy Google's AI Chips
**Published:** 28 October 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-plans-buy-googles-ai-chips-information-reports-2026-10-28/)

OpenAI reportedly plans to purchase Google's Tensor Processing Units (TPUs),
according to The Information. The potential deal would deepen ties between
the two companies and give OpenAI another option for AI computing alongside
Nvidia GPUs. Financial terms have not been disclosed, and the reported plan
has not been confirmed by either company.

**Why it matters:** Highlights the importance of hardware choice, inference
costs, and cloud infrastructure in scaling AI applications.

### 2. Cognizant in Talks to Invest in Indian AI Startup Sarvam
**Published:** 28 October 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/startups/cognizant-in-talks-to-invest-in-indian-ai-startup-sarvam-valuing-it-at-3-5-4-billion-sources/articleshow/135240139.cms)

Cognizant is reportedly in talks to participate in Sarvam's latest funding
round, which could value the Indian AI startup at $3.5–4 billion. Sarvam is
reportedly seeking around $300 million in new funding. The discussions do
not constitute a completed investment.

**Why it matters:** Reflects growing investment in Indian AI startups and
the demand for locally developed models and enterprise AI solutions.

### 3. Sarvam Signs AI Agreement with India's Ministry of Defence
**Published:** 27 October 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/ai/ai-insights/sarvam-enters-defence-ai-with-projects-across-army-navy-and-air-force/amp_articleshow/135227528.cms)

Sarvam signed an MoU with Headquarters Integrated Defence Staff to develop
AI applications for the Indian Army, Navy, and Air Force. Planned work
includes decision support, training, knowledge management, target acquisition,
and sensor-data fusion, with human oversight over operational decisions.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates the use of sovereign AI infrastructure,
foundation models, and agentic systems in sensitive applications.

### 4. Liquid AI Launches LFM3 Multimodal Model Family
**Published:** 27 October 2026  
**Source:** [Liquid AI](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm3)

Liquid AI introduced LFM3, a family of open-weight models covering text,
vision, audio, multimodal interaction, and structured decisions. Its
compact models are designed for local deployment and fine-tuning.

**Why it matters:** Smaller models can reduce inference costs and enable
privacy-friendly AI applications on local devices.

### 5. Google DeepMind Introduces Genie 4 for Interactive World Simulation
**Published:** 27 October 2026  
**Source:** [Google DeepMind](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/introducing-genie-4/)

Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, a world model that generates interactive
environments in real time. Users can steer scenes through natural-language
instructions, and the model generates visuals and audio. Access has begun
with a limited group of trusted testers.

**Why it matters:** Interactive world models could support robotics,
autonomous agents, gaming, and simulation-based AI training.
