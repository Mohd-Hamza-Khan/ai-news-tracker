## 📰 Latest AI News — 29 October 2026

### 1. DeepSeek and Moonshot Reportedly Used OpenAI Model Outputs to Train Their Latest Models
**Published:** 29 October 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/deepseek-moonshot-used-openais-gpt-6-astra-train-latest-models-information-2026-10-29/)

A report cited by Reuters says DeepSeek and Moonshot AI relied heavily on outputs from OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra to train newer models. The reported practice, known as knowledge distillation, can help developers reproduce advanced model capabilities at lower cost. The companies had not responded to Reuters' requests for comment at publication time.

**Why it matters:** Highlights model distillation, training-data provenance, and the competitive dynamics of frontier AI development.

### 2. Google Launches Gemini 4 Carbon with a Cybersecurity Focus
**Published:** 28 October 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/google-launches-gemini-4-carbon-ai-model-with-cybersecurity-focus-2026-10-28/)

Google introduced Gemini 4 Carbon, a frontier model focused on advanced coding and cybersecurity tasks, including vulnerability discovery and software patching. Initial access is limited to selected cybersecurity defenders through Google's Fairwind Program.

**Why it matters:** Shows how frontier models are expanding into code security, vulnerability analysis, and automated remediation.

### 3. Google DeepMind Unveils Genie 4 for Interactive World Simulation
**Published:** 27 October 2026  
**Source:** [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/)

Genie 4 generates interactive simulated worlds with realistic movement, native audio, and real-time changes driven by natural-language prompts. Potential uses include robotics training, game prototyping, creative workflows, and agent evaluation.

**Why it matters:** World models could provide simulated environments for developing and testing AI agents.

### 4. Liquid AI Releases LFM3, a Family of Compact Multimodal Models
**Published:** 27 October 2026  
**Source:** [Liquid AI](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm3)

Liquid AI introduced LFM3, an open-weight model family covering text, vision, audio, multimodal interaction, and structured decisions. The models are designed for local deployment and specialised fine-tuning.

**Why it matters:** Compact models can help developers build lower-latency, cost-efficient, and privacy-conscious AI applications.

### 5. Nearly 60% of Surveyed Firms Have Yet to Move AI Beyond Pilots
**Published:** 28 October 2026  
**Source:** [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/artificial-intelligence-and-automation/news)

A Citi Institute report found that nearly 60% of surveyed organisations remained in the exploration or pilot stages of AI adoption, while around 40% had progressed to production or scaling.

**Why it matters:** Demonstrates why production AI engineering requires evaluation, monitoring, cost control, security, and reliable deployment—not just a successful prototype.
