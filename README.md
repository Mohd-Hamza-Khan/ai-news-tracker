## 📰 Latest AI News — 9 November 2026

### 1. Anthropic Introduces Claude Opus 5.5
**Published:** 8 November 2026  
**Source:** [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news)

Anthropic announced Claude Opus 5.5, describing it as its strongest model for agentic coding and knowledge work. The company says it costs approximately 40% less to run than Opus 5 on typical workloads.

**Why it matters:** More capable and cost-efficient models can improve coding agents, multi-step workflows, and AI application economics.

### 2. Anthropic Reportedly Secures $3.4 Billion in Gas-Powered Generators for AI Data Centres
**Published:** 8 November 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/anthropic-secures-34-billion-generator-deal-ai-data-centers-ft-reports-2026-11-08/)

Anthropic reportedly agreed to a $3.4 billion deal to secure 1.7 gigawatts of gas-fired generators for its data centres. Reuters could not independently verify the report.

**Why it matters:** Electricity and data-centre infrastructure are becoming critical constraints for scaling AI models and services.

### 3. South Korea Says Nvidia GPU Deliveries Will Continue Despite Controversy
**Published:** 9 November 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-korea-says-nvidia-issues-wont-affect-contracted-gpu-supplies-2026-11-09/)

South Korea says Nvidia GPU deliveries under existing contracts are expected to continue. The government confirmed that 10,000 GPUs funded through a previous budget had already arrived and were in use.

**Why it matters:** GPU availability directly affects AI training, inference costs, and large-scale deployment.

### 4. US AI Safety Centre Faces Questions About Its Independence
**Published:** 9 November 2026  
**Source:** [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/d3318eb2cf011df53e9ddb3e03d25f13)

The US government's AI safety centre faces concerns over leadership, research activity, and its independence. Reports of possible informal reporting to a White House office have raised questions about independent AI evaluations.

**Why it matters:** Independent safety testing and transparent model evaluations are increasingly important as AI agents become more autonomous.

### 5. Trump Signals Openness to AI Safety Guardrails
**Published:** 9 November 2026  
**Source:** [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-says-ai-risks-can-be-managed-signals-openness-guardrails-2026-11-09/)

Donald Trump said he is open to AI safeguards and could consider working with Democrats on protections, while emphasising the importance of US competitiveness against China.

**Why it matters:** AI policy can shape how developers build, evaluate, deploy, and monitor AI systems.

## 📌 How News Is Selected

Each news entry should include:

- **Headline:** What happened?
- **Summary:** What are the key details?

News should be verified against reliable sources before publication.
