## ✅ Verification Checklist

Before publishing a news story:

- [ ] Open the original article or official announcement; do not rely only on a search snippet.
- [ ] Verify the original publication date and distinguish it from any update date.
- [ ] Prefer primary sources for model details, pricing, availability, and product claims.
- [ ] Label funding talks, leaks, and reported plans as unconfirmed when appropriate.
- [ ] Attribute benchmark results and company claims; don't describe them as independently verified unless they are.
- [ ] Cross-check major claims with a reputable independent source.
- [ ] Write a concise summary in your own words.
- [ ] Explain why the development matters to AI engineers or the AI industry.
- [ ] Include a direct link to the original source.
- [ ] Check `README.md` and the archive for duplicate stories.
- [ ] Keep the five newest stories in `README.md` and move older stories to `news.md`.

## 📅 Recommended Daily Workflow

1. Check ET/ETtech for Indian AI policy, startups, funding, and enterprise news.
2. Scan Reuters and other reputable technology newsrooms for global developments.
3. Check official AI company blogs to confirm announcements and technical details.
4. Browse arXiv, Hugging Face, and GitHub for research and open-source releases.
5. Select the five most important stories, record their true publication dates, and include source links.
